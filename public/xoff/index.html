If you read the news

Start your day with something that shows you stories in the order they were published. SOURCE! is a free news reader built on RSS, so the independent outlets and journalists you pick appear newest first, and nothing is boosted because it's controversial.

Try building a feed of the outlets you would read instead of scrolling X. In SOURCE! it's called an RSS Pack. Feeds start out private, and you can share yours if you want to.

Get it on iPhone and iPad Open it in your browser

On Android? The app is in closed beta, and testers are what let it launch publicly. It takes two minutes:

  1. Join the beta group with the Google account on your phone's Play Store.
  2. Then open the Play testing link and tap "Become a tester." Joining the group alone doesn't count.
  3. Install it from the Play Store and open it once. Please stay opted in for two weeks.

If you want politicians to change

Candidates break news on X because that's where reporters are. Voters can change that by asking, and a message from a constituent counts for more than a hashtag. Copy this, add your district, and send it to your representatives and the candidates on your ballot.

I'm a voter in [your city or district]. Until every vote is counted, please announce news and make statements on your own website or newsletter first, and on Bluesky, instead of X. X's owner is spending heavily to elect Republicans this year, and I'd like to read what you have to say without his platform deciding who sees it. Thank you.

Not sure who represents you? usa.gov/elected-officials lists them and how to contact them. The same ask applies to Facebook and Instagram. Posting on their own site or newsletter first, where anyone can read it with no algorithm in between, is the point.

Why X, specifically

X's owner is one of the biggest political spenders in modern American history. Elon Musk gave more than $291 million in the 2024 cycle, according to OpenSecrets, making him the largest donor of that cycle. The Washington Post's analysis of the filings found it was the largest total from a single donor since at least 2010, outside of candidates funding their own campaigns.

He is back this year. Reports this summer put what he has personally given toward the midterms above $85 million, and say he authorized $100 million to $120 million for his America PAC's program.

The platform matters as well as the money. A study in Nature found that X's algorithm promotes MAGA content and demotes traditional media, nudging users' attitudes to the right.

Figures come from reporting through early autumn 2026 and will change as new filings come in.

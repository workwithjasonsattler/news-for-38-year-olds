What makes SOURCE! different

Six things SOURCE! does that a plain RSS reader leaves to you.

01

You start with a full feed

Handpicked RSS Packs of independent journalism give you a morning feed in under a minute. Never heard of RSS? You don’t need to have. Every Pack has its own page you can share, copy and follow.

02

Every change is yours, and you can see it

Pick top sources, show fewer stories from a source, hide one, mute a word. Switch on Sort by relevance when you want stories your sources agree on first, with the outlets named. Newest first stays the default.

03

Support goes with the story

Where a journalist or publication has a tip jar or subscribe page, one tap takes you there. The money goes to them, not through us.

04

Videos and podcasts, newest first

Paste a YouTube link or @handle and the channel joins your videos. Paste a podcast’s RSS link and the show joins your podcasts. Everything comes out in the order it was posted, and nothing is recommended to you.

05

Buzz without the scoreboard

See what journalists are saying on Bluesky. SOURCE! shows the words and the names and leaves out the like and repost counts.

06

Leave whenever you like

Import your feeds from another reader, export them as OPML any time, and read without an account. There are no ads and no trackers.

Roadmap

Where this is heading as of now.

1. The promise
NOW

Real-time reporting from independent outlets, and an easy way to support the people behind it. Live on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web.

2. Your feeds
NEXT

Create and share feeds of the publications you support or the subjects you follow, and tune your main feed until it’s yours.

3. Reporting and conversation
THEN

Connect original reporting to the Buzz, podcasts and videos on the same subject.

4. Indie journalist store
SOMEDAY

A place to find and support independent journalists directly, part Substack alternative, part browsing a store for shows you didn’t know you needed.

How it works

Real sources, in order

Every story comes straight from the outlet's own RSS feed, newest first. Nothing is boosted because it's controversial. Nothing is hidden to keep you scrolling.

Build your own RSS Packs

Collect the outlets you trust or the subjects you follow into a Pack. Share it, copy someone else's, or export it as an open RSS feed. It's yours, not a platform's.

Support the reporter

Where an outlet has a tip jar or subscribe page, one tap takes you straight to it. Specific support for specific people, sent directly to them.

Roadmap

Where this is going, in four steps. It's a direction, not a promise. Tell us which step you want first.

1. The promise
NOW

Easy-to-follow, real-time reporting from independent outlets, and an easy way to support the people behind it. Live on iPhone and iPad, in closed beta on Android.

2. Your feeds
NEXT

Create and share feeds of the publications you support or the subjects you follow. Train your main feed until it's exactly what you want, with room to discover new voices.

3. Reporting and conversation
THEN

Connect original reporting to the Buzz, podcasts, and videos on the same subject, so a story and everything around it live in one place.

4. Indie journalist store
SOMEDAY

A place to find and support independent journalists directly, part Substack alternative, part browsing a store for shows you didn't know you needed.

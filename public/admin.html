BULK ADD FEEDS

Paste one feed per line, fields separated by |:
Outlet | Feed URL | Section | Default Author | Tip URL | Subscribe URL | Bluesky Handle | Type | YouTube Channel
Outlet is always required, and you need at least one of a Feed URL, a Bluesky Handle, or a YouTube Channel — leave the rest blank if you don't have them, e.g. Garbage Day | https://rss.beehiiv.com/feeds/99VTTjt0N1.xml | Indie Media. To add a person who only has a Bluesky handle and no RSS feed, just leave the Feed URL field blank. Section must be exactly Indie Media, Data Centers, or People Power (defaults to Indie Media if left blank or misspelled). Bluesky Handle can be a bare handle (marisakabas.bsky.social) or a full profile URL. Leave Type blank for a normal Organization — write individual (or journalist, same thing) to add a Bluesky-only person who never gets a wire column and only shows up in Sources. YouTube Channel can be a handle (@channelname), raw channel ID, or full URL.
